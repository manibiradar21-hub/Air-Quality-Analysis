🌫️ India Air Quality Analytics —

India Air Quality Analytics is an end-to-end Data Analytics and Business Intelligence project developed to analyze air pollution patterns across cities and states in India. The project transforms a raw air-quality dataset containing 10,000 observations into a structured analytical dataset and an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps identify pollution trends, high-risk locations, seasonal patterns, and relationships between different air pollutants and Air Quality Index (AQI).
The project began with a raw dataset containing information such as city, state, monitoring station, date and time, AQI, PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃, temperature, humidity, and wind speed. Python and Pandas were used for data preprocessing, including identifying and handling missing values, removing duplicate records, validating numerical values, converting date fields, and preparing the dataset for analysis. Additional analytical features such as Year, Month, Quarter, Day, Day of Week, Season, and AQI Category were created to support time-based and categorical analysis.
Following data preparation, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) was performed using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to understand the distribution and behavior of AQI and individual pollutants. The analysis examined city-wise and state-wise pollution levels, monthly and yearly AQI trends, seasonal variations, pollutant distributions, extreme AQI observations, and relationships between AQI and environmental factors. Correlation analysis was also performed to identify which pollutants have stronger statistical relationships with AQI.
The cleaned dataset was then integrated into Microsoft Power BI to create an interactive dark-themed Air Quality & Pollution Analytics dashboard. The dashboard provides KPI cards for Average AQI, Maximum AQI, Average PM2.5, Average PM10, Total Cities, and Total Records. Interactive slicers allow users to filter the analysis by Year, State, City, Season, Month, and AQI Category.
The dashboard includes visualizations for monthly AQI trends, top polluted cities, state-wise average AQI, AQI category distribution, seasonal pollution patterns, pollutant trends, PM2.5 versus AQI relationships, and city-level pollution comparisons. These visualizations allow users to quickly identify areas with higher pollution levels and understand how air quality changes across different periods and geographical locations.
The project also includes a Key Insights section that summarizes important findings from the analysis, including the overall AQI level, most polluted city, worst-performing season, highest PM2.5 city, strongest pollutant-AQI correlation, and the percentage of observations falling into poor or worse AQI categories.

- Analyze air-quality conditions across Indian cities and states.
- Identify cities and regions with higher pollution levels.
- Understand monthly, yearly, and seasonal AQI patterns.
- Analyze individual pollutant concentrations.
- Study relationships between pollutants and AQI.
- Categorize observations based on AQI severity.
- Transform raw data into an analysis-ready dataset.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for data-driven insights.
- Present complex environmental data through clear and business-friendly visualizations.
